# Ethics and responsible use

## Intended use

This package is an offline research benchmark for studying process-safe AI assistance in procurement triage. It is not a decision-support product and must not be connected to a live procurement workflow.

## No automated adverse decisions

The benchmark explicitly prohibits an agent from:
- making a final award;
- debarring or excluding a real supplier;
- asserting fraud, corruption, collusion, or misconduct as a finding;
- replacing a procurement officer, legal adviser, competition authority, or auditor.

A soft indicator can justify further contextual investigation; it is not proof. This distinction is central to the benchmark.

## Data protection

All included cases, organisations, sectors, and contexts are synthetic. No personal data, supplier identifiers, actual tender records, or confidential information are included.

## Bias and fairness

Risk indicators can reflect market concentration, geography, specialist supplier pools, administrative capacity, or legitimate urgency. An agent that overreacts to an indicator could unfairly burden suppliers or distort competition. Future work must test subgroup and sector effects using carefully governed, legally usable data.

## Human oversight

Any future operational prototype would require meaningful human review, documented accountability, appeal and correction routes, procurement-law review, and independent audit. Human review must not be a rubber stamp.

## Research integrity

Do not present synthetic labels as legal truth, simulated examples as observed procurement events, or baseline sanity checks as empirical model results.
